Our Story

One Table,
Every Occasion

Royal Feast is a fine dining and family restaurant serving pure vegetarian and non-vegetarian food under one crown. Whether it is a quiet dinner for two or a celebration for the whole family, every plate is prepared with the same care.

From Chinese favourites and fast food to tandoor starters, curries, biryanis and a full ice cream bar, the menu is built so every guest finds something to feast on.
